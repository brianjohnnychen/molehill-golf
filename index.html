Meet Divot

Divot lives under the 18th green. Every time a putt drops, he pops up to see who made it. He is on every Molehill bag, ball and tee, so a kid can find their own gear from across the range.

Divot's rule for every product we make: if it is too heavy for him to drag into the hole, it is too heavy for a six-year-old.
